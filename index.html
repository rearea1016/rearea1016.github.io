ONGOING RESEARCH

ANTiaromatic NORCORROLE · SAM · EGaIn

How do metal substrates change charge and thermoelectric transport across an antiaromatic molecular junction?

Antiaromatic molecules offer an unusual hypothesis: a narrow HOMO–LUMO gap may help escape the familiar trade-off between electrical conductance and the Seebeck coefficient.
